Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions or need help with your journey? We’re here to assist.

What devices can run Wizard’s Journey?

Wizard’s Journey is optimized for all modern iPhone and iPad models running iOS 16 or later, featuring smooth 60+ FPS performance.

Does the game support Bluetooth controllers?

Yes! In addition to our intuitive on-screen twin-stick virtual controls, full MFi, Xbox, and PlayStation wireless controllers are supported natively.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Absolutely. All core runs, biomes, and endless survival can be enjoyed completely offline whenever you’re traveling.

Transparency

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here is how Wizard's Journey handles data.

Overview

Odder Otter Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through our game, Wizard's Journey.

Data Collection & Use

Wizard's Journey is designed to minimize data collection. We do not collect or store personal identifying information such as your name, physical address, or phone number.

  • Game Progress: Saved game data, unlocked spells, and achievements are stored locally on your device or in your personal Apple iCloud storage (if enabled).
  • Crash & Diagnostic Data: Anonymized diagnostic logs may be collected through standard Apple developer services to help us patch crashes and improve performance.
  • Advertisements: Optional rewarded video ads (such as continuing a run or bonus gold) are served using Google AdMob in accordance with Google and Apple privacy standards.

Children's Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@odderotter.online.
